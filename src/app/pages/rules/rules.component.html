Rules of Romanian Whist

Objective of the Game

The objective of Romanian Whist is to correctly predict the number of tricks you will win during a round. It is a strategic and intuitive game played with 3 to 7 players.

Setup

  • The game uses a 32-card deck (only the 7, 8, 9, 10, Jack, Queen, King, and Ace are in play).
  • The number of rounds is determined by the number of cards dealt in each hand. For example, with 4 players, there are 8 rounds (starting with one card and increasing up to 8 cards).
  • The dealer rotates between players, and the dealer’s role changes every round.

Bidding Phase

  1. After the cards are dealt, each player predicts how many tricks they expect to win. The predictions are made in a clockwise direction, starting with the player to the left of the dealer.
  2. The bid can range from 0 to the number of tricks possible in the round. If a player bids 0, they are predicting they will win no tricks at all, and if they bid 8, they are predicting they will win every trick in the round.
  3. Each player's bid is a crucial part of the strategy, as it influences the play during the trick-taking phase.

Gameplay

  1. The dealer starts the first trick by playing any card from their hand.
  2. Players must follow the suit of the card played if they can. If they cannot follow the suit, they may play any other card (including a trump card, if there is a trump suit).
  3. The winner of the trick is the player who plays the highest card in the leading suit or the highest trump card, if a trump suit is in play.
  4. If no trump suit is declared, the highest card in the lead suit wins the trick.
  5. Players continue playing until all tricks in the round are completed.

Scoring

  • The scoring is based on whether players fulfill their bids. If a player wins the exact number of tricks they predicted, they score points equal to their bid.
  • If a player wins more or fewer tricks than they predicted, they score 0 points.
  • At the end of the game, the player or team with the highest score wins.
  • The total number of rounds and the number of tricks per round increases with each hand, so players must adjust their strategies accordingly.

Trump Suit

The trump suit (if applicable) is determined by the first card turned up after dealing. The trump suit is stronger than all other suits, so trump cards win over non-trump cards in a trick.

  • If the first card turned over is a Spade, Spades are the trump suit for that round.
  • The trump suit does not change throughout the round.

Winning the Game

The game is typically played until a player or team reaches a predetermined number of points, such as 50 or 100. The player/team that reaches the target score first is declared the winner.
